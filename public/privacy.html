Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Bradley-Fuller, Inc, doing business as Ginger Sue's ("Bradley-Fuller, Inc," "Ginger Sue's," "we," "us"), runs restaurants in Lee's Summit, MO, Liberty, MO and Olathe, KS. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information in the Ginger Sue's Schedule app at schedule.gingersues.com and in the text messages Ginger Sue's sends to its team members about their work schedules.

Who this applies to

The Ginger Sue's Schedule app and Ginger Sue's schedule text messages are for Ginger Sue's employees and managers. They are not offered to the public.

Information we collect

  • Contact and work details you or your manager provide: your name, mobile phone number, work locations, job positions, availability and maximum hours.
  • Scheduling activity: your shifts, time-off requests, shift pickup, drop and cover requests, and messages posted in the app.
  • Pay rates entered by managers, which only Ginger Sue's managers and owners can see.
  • Sign-in information: your mobile number is used to send you a one-time sign-in code. We keep a record of when you sign in.
  • Text message records: the content, time and delivery status of schedule texts we send you.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the app securely, such as error logs.

How we use it

  • To build, publish and show work schedules, and to handle time-off and shift requests.
  • To let you sign in with a one-time code sent to your phone.
  • To send you text messages about your schedule: new schedules, changes to your shifts, decisions on your time-off requests, and shift-cover requests from coworkers.
  • To keep the app secure and working.

We do not use your information for advertising.

Text messages (SMS)

You receive Ginger Sue's schedule text messages because you gave your mobile number to Ginger Sue's as part of your employment and sign in to the Ginger Sue's Schedule app. Message frequency varies with your schedule. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts at any time, or HELP for help. See the SMS Terms in our Terms & Conditions.

Bradley-Fuller, Inc (Ginger Sue's): We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Bradley-Fuller, Inc will not share mobile information or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that run the app for us, and only so they can provide that service:

  • Google (Firebase) hosts the app and its data and sends sign-in codes.
  • Twilio delivers our schedule text messages.

We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see what

Team members can see the published schedule, the team list (names and positions) and messages. Only you and Ginger Sue's managers can see your time-off requests. Pay rates and phone numbers are visible only to Ginger Sue's managers and owners.

How long we keep it

We keep schedule and account information while you work at Ginger Sue's and for as long as needed for business and legal records afterward. When you leave, your sign-in access is turned off.

Security

Information is stored with Google Firebase, protected by sign-in and access rules that limit what each person can see.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any Ginger Sue's text to stop schedule texts. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Ask your manager to update or correct your information, or to remove your mobile number.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
